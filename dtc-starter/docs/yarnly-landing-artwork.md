# Yarnly landing artwork

- Generator: built-in imagegen tool (not the CLI fallback).
- Website asset: `apps/storefront/public/images/yarnly-pixel-garden.webp`.
- Original generation: 1672 × 941 PNG. The website copy keeps those dimensions and is encoded as WebP at quality 90 (460,728 bytes).
- Usage: full-bleed hero and footer illustration on `/vn/ve-yarnly`.
- Reference direction: the pixel landscape, blue sky, green scenery and placement of copy in the CoFounder design shown at https://www.lapa.ninja/post/cofounder-2/.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: full-bleed website hero illustration, wide 16:9 high resolution. Create a beautiful crisp 16-bit pixel art landscape with a bright blue summer sky, large blocky white clouds, a peaceful green park and distant modest Vietnamese city skyline along the low left horizon. A huge leafy tree and its dark shaded trunk frame the RIGHT side. In the LOWER RIGHT foreground under the tree, an artisan crochet picnic: a woven basket of cream, coral, sage and pale-blue yarn balls, wooden crochet hooks, an adorable half-finished crocheted teddy bear, tiny crocheted flowers, and a folded cream crochet blanket lying on grass. The upper-left 55 percent MUST be uninterrupted medium blue sky with very few clouds, deliberately empty for a white website heading. All craft objects must occupy only the lower-right 40 percent. Wide horizontal landscape composition, rich layered foliage, little sunlit wildflowers, clean hand-placed square pixels and stair-step edges, retro adventure-game environment, atmospheric depth, bright welcoming mood. Artwork fills the whole image edge to edge. No typography, no letters, no text, no logos, no UI, no laptop, no computer, no people, no watermark. Do not make a web page mockup; only the background artwork.
